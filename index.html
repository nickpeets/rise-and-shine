The session

The class is the waitlist.

Portland's weekend brunch lines are an hour long because a table is the only thing on offer. Here you book a two-and-a-half-hour arc, and the table is the end of it. Sixteen people start together on the hour.

First 50 minutes

Move

A slow flow upstairs. Beginners welcome; nobody's checking your form.

Next 60 minutes

Heat & cold

Two groups of eight rotate through the cedar sauna and the plunge. A sauna host keeps time. Sober until the table.

Last 40 minutes

Eat

Your seat is already set. Brunch, tea, coffee, a mimosa if you want one. A DJ keeps the room at a hum.

$79 per session, all three parts. Members book first and pay less. Pilot pricing

The day

Same person, different timelines.

The person who closed a bar at 2 a.m. and the person three years sober want the same Sunday: movement, heat, good food, other people. The day is built so both show up and neither feels like a guest in someone else's room.

Drinks

Equal billing.

The place

The old Amalfi's, 47th & Fremont.

A corner building, a brick cottage, and a yard, which is the whole concept in three rooms. Crunchy and clean at once: cedar, wool blankets, hand-painted signs, a piano somebody's uncle gave us.

  • UpstairsThe yoga studio. Sixteen mats, wood floor, east light.
  • Main floorBrunch room and bar. Booths from the old days, a DJ by the window.
  • The cottageTeahouse. Opens at seven. The quiet room all day.
  • The yardCedar sauna, plunge, fire pit, and the piano under cover. Where the afternoon happens.
